DESCRIPTION OF COURSE

  • Digital economics is about the management of economics using a ICT
  • Maths for computer science is the building of mathematical concepts used in a numerical manner
  • Web programming is the management of machine languages using a numerical manner to create web pages or websites to provide services
  • Arduino is the study of microcontrollers and microprocessors based on their applications in embedded systems
  • Algorithm is the study of algorithms and their applications (how to use it) in computer science
  • Information system is a course which help us to more understand the computer hardware and software components
